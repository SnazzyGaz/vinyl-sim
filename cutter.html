    Notes on the cut
    • The cut runs on your computer: through serve.py in the local app, or right in the browser on the live page. Your audio is never uploaded anywhere else.
    • The groove master WAV is the prepared groove itself (mono, filtered, normalised), not a copy of your upload. Keep it and the JSON beside the PNG under the same name to play from the vector master.
    • A PNG on its own still plays, traced from its pixels, but it is deliberately lo-fi rather than MP3 quality. A 4096 map cannot hold a whole LP of full-band grooves.
    • The viewing picture is an 8-bit image with the paper label; it is not a record and the Player will refuse it.
