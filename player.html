Handling and notes
  • Needle: grab the brass arm and lift it to stop, lower it onto the record to play (a click on the arm toggles it). The Drop / Lift button does the same. Click a groove on the disc to drop it there.
  • Speed and volume: use the cabinet rail — 33⅓ / 45 / 78 or the fine knob, and the volume knob.
  • Sleeves: loaded records wait in sleeves beside the cabinet — drag one onto the platter to swap. The record you were playing goes back in its sleeve.
  • Scratch: hold the disc to stop it; drag around the centre to scratch back and forth. Let go and it plays on from there.
  • Position: the record turns under a fixed needle. Moving Position while the needle is lifted will not drop it.
  • View: wheel zooms a little, right-drag swings round the front of the machine, middle-drag dollies. Reset view restores the camera.
  • Speed: 33⅓ / 45 / 78 or the fine knob. Pitch and duration change together.
  • Sources: with its groove WAV and JSON the record plays from the vector master; a PNG alone is traced from its pixels and is deliberately lo-fi. The 8-bit viewing picture is not a record.
  • This is a digital stylus, not a guarantee that a physical print would play. Mono, no RIAA. Files stay on this computer; the 3D turntable needs the Three.js library from the internet.
