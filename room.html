    Handling
    
  
    
    
    Cut a record
    Drop a song anywhere, or click the stack of blanks on the bench (O). A picture dropped with it becomes the cover. Up to 4:30. The finished record is filed in the crate; records you open are filed there too.
    
    
    Play
    Carry a sleeve from the crate over the cabinet and let go when the rim glows; the empty sleeve goes back in the crate. Lift or lower the arm, or press Space.
    
    
    Shelf
    Every record you cut or open stands in the crate by the machine and is still there next visit. Hover the crate to browse titles; drag one to the gramophone to play it. Delete takes the hovered record off the shelf.
    
    
    Sleeves
    Put them down anywhere on the floor; they stay. Back into the crate, or onto the post tray to save.
    
    
    Inspect
    With the needle up, pick the record off the platter (I). Drag to turn it, scroll to look closer; Esc puts it back.
    
    
    Scratch
    While playing, hold the record and drag round the centre.
    
    
    View
    Right-drag to walk round the front, wheel to move closer. Home goes back to the default seat.
